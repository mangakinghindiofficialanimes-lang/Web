SITE INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

Starter template — review and update before public launch.

Information this site may process

When connected to Supabase, account email addresses and profile roles may be stored to provide account and admin functions. The site may record catalogue views, video plays and download clicks for basic site analytics. This starter does not intentionally request precise location or collect payment information.

Why data is used

Account data is used to authenticate users and protect administrative functions. Activity events are used to understand which catalogue entries are viewed or played and to troubleshoot the site.

Data access and security

Database access is intended to be restricted using Supabase Row Level Security. Site operators with authorised administrative access may see profile and activity data. No online service can promise absolute security; configure and test access rules before launch.

Retention and requests

This starter does not define a final retention schedule or automated account export/deletion request process. The site owner must add a contact process, retention rules and any notices required for their audience and location before public launch.

Contact

For site questions, contact mythicsframestelegram@gmail.com.

This is a starter notice, not legal advice. Update it to match the site's real practices and applicable requirements before collecting real users' information.
