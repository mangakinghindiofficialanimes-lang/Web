RESPONSIBLE PUBLISHING

Media policy.

Starter policy — complete the contact and removal process before launch.

Only authorised media

Only publish videos, artwork, subtitles, and download links that you created, own, licensed, or have clear permission to distribute. A title being popular or available elsewhere does not mean it can be re-uploaded or redistributed.

Source links

Administrators must check that each video and download URL is permitted for the intended use and that the licence allows streaming, downloading, and the countries served. Do not bypass provider restrictions or access controls.

Requests and corrections

If you believe content listed here is unauthorised, contact the site operator at mythicsframestelegram@gmail.com with the page URL, the material concerned, and an explanation of your request. This template is not a substitute for a legally reviewed takedown process.

Sample catalogue

Sample titles and abstract artwork in this starter are illustrative design placeholders. No actual episode media is bundled with the project.
